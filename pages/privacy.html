1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to visitors and customers using the Tom Basco Enterprise website and services.

By using our website, you acknowledge that your information may be handled as described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

We may collect the following information when you browse our website, request a quote or place an order:

  • Contact details, such as your name, telephone number and email address.
  • Delivery information, including your location, address and nearby landmarks.
  • Order information, including products, quantities and purchase details.
  • Payment confirmation details needed to verify your payment.
  • Website usage information, such as pages visited and general browsing activity, where collected.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to enquiries and quote requests.
  • Confirm product availability, prices and orders.
  • Arrange deliveries and customer collections.
  • Verify payments and prevent payment errors.
  • Contact you about your order or any changes affecting it.
  • Improve our website and customer service.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share relevant information with delivery providers or service providers where necessary to fulfil your order, operate our website or provide our services.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or a lawful request from the relevant authorities.

5. Payments

Payments are made through Mobile Money using the payment details provided during the ordering process.

We may use payment confirmation information to verify that your payment has been received.

We will never ask you to provide your Mobile Money PIN or password.

6. Cookies and website use

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality and improve your browsing experience.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work as intended if cookies are disabled.

7. How we protect your information

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we collect against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no method of storing or transmitting information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, including order management, customer service and applicable legal requirements.

9. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate information or raise concerns about how your information is being used.

To make a request, please contact us using the details provided below.

10. Third-party links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. These websites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices.

11. Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for general customers and businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorisation.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
