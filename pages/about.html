Our story

Tom Basco Enterprise is an Interplast agent based at Adum Aseda House in Kumasi. We stock PVC pipes, fittings, drainage and water system products, so most jobs can be supplied from one shop.

Whether you are plumbing a new house, laying drainage on a site or fixing a leak at home, our team helps you choose the right size and quantity, and gets it to you the same day around Kumasi and its environs.
